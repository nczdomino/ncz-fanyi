Modifications made by this project:
  - only Mandarin sentences of 4-40 characters that contain a headword from the bundled
    dictionary, and that have at least one translation, are kept
  - at most three examples per headword, ranked by position/length/difficulty/translation
  - collocations are derived counts (which dictionary words appear within 4 characters of
    the headword), not text from Tatoeba
  - stored as {"word": {"e": [[zh, vi, ja, en], ...], "c": [[collocation, count], ...]}}
